# Provisional player combat

The combat module is independent of the window and transport layers. `RocketDefinitions` reads the rocket rows in `chasm.inf`, preserving their numeric fields and using the model name and power. The game's modding manual says rockets without a 3D model are instant. In the installed data, the first three weapon slots use instant rockets (IDs 3, 11, and 7); slots 4, 5, and 8 now launch provisional physical projectiles. Grenade and mine slots remain unsupported without consuming ammunition.

A `Combat` state keeps a cooldown shared across weapon changes. A successful shot consumes one unit of the selected weapon's ammunition, uses a horizontal ray for instant shots or emits a physical launch for supported projectile weapons. Instant shots test shot-blocking walls and damage the nearest living actor intersecting the ray. `ShotImpact` reports a physical launch or whether an instant shot hit a wall, an actor, or nothing; actor events include source index, damage, death, and distance. Health is clamped to zero and actors are kept in the list, preserving stable placement IDs. Unsupported, unowned, empty, cooling-down, or invalid shots do not consume ammunition.

This is a gameplay scaffold, not verified original behavior. Reload time is provisionally divided by 256 to produce seconds (with a 1/60-second minimum); the hit circle uses the actor's map-wall radius in map units divided by 256, with conservative bounds; range is 64 tiles. Multi-projectile weapons currently apply `power × projectile_count` on one ray without spread. Collision is horizontal only, with no vertical targeting, meshes, head shots, breakable objects, splash, ricochets, faithful projectile physics, accurate animation timing, or authoritative multiplayer transport. The original game may use different ammo and reload rules, projectile spread, damage, actor hit boxes and distance behavior.

The 21 rocket definitions and all eight weapon-to-rocket references were checked against the installed full game's archive. Synthetic tests cover nearest actor selection, cooldown and ammo, death, wall occlusion and pass-through, invalid shots, and malformed rocket tables.

Session instant fire supports positive-up pitch using the player's eye height. Object model bounds clip the ray through each object's horizontal cylinder; unknown heights remain blocking. Actors currently use a provisional 0–2 tile vertical body and planes use provisional flat room bounds. Shots stop at the first floor or ceiling plane, currently reported as a miss. Horizontal legacy `Combat` APIs remain available.
