# Provisional player combat

The combat module is independent of the window and transport layers. `RocketDefinitions` reads the rocket rows in `chasm.inf`, preserving their numeric fields and using the model name and power. The game's modding manual says rockets without a 3D model are instant. In the installed data, the first three weapon slots use instant rockets (IDs 3, 11, and 7); the other five use physical projectiles and currently return `Unsupported`, without consuming ammunition.

A `Combat` state keeps a cooldown shared across weapon changes. A successful shot consumes one unit of the selected weapon's ammunition, sends a horizontal ray from the player, tests shot-blocking walls with a caller-provided material predicate, and then damages the nearest living actor intersecting the ray. `ShotImpact` reports whether a wall, actor, or nothing was hit; actor events include source index, damage, death, and distance. Health is clamped to zero and actors are kept in the list, preserving stable placement IDs. Unsupported, unowned, empty, cooling-down, or invalid shots do not consume ammunition.

This is a gameplay scaffold, not verified original behavior. Reload time is provisionally divided by 256 to produce seconds (with a 1/60-second minimum); the hit circle uses the actor's map-wall radius in map units divided by 256, with conservative bounds; range is 64 tiles. Multi-projectile weapons currently apply `power × projectile_count` on one ray without spread. Collision is horizontal only, with no vertical targeting, meshes, head shots, breakable objects, splash, ricochets, projectiles, animation, sound, or authoritative multiplayer transport. The original game may use different ammo and reload rules, projectile spread, damage, actor hit boxes and distance behavior.

The 21 rocket definitions and all eight weapon-to-rocket references were checked against the installed full game's archive. Synthetic tests cover nearest actor selection, cooldown and ammo, death, wall occlusion and pass-through, invalid shots, and malformed rocket tables.
