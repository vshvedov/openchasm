# Player pickup research

The bundled modding manual documents object `ACode` values: 1–8 are weapons; 10–89 are ammunition; 102 is health, 120 is big health, 124 is a helmet, 128 is armor, and 141–143 are red, green and blue keys. Codes 131–133 are timed items and 255 is a switch. These come from item and level-object definitions rather than fixed model filenames.

The `WEAPONS` section of `chasm.inf` specifies, among other fields, ammunition per portion (`DAm`), ammunition limit (`Limit`), and amount supplied when picking up a weapon (`Start`). The independent [PanzerChasm player implementation](https://github.com/Panzerschrek/Chasm-Reverse/blob/master/src/server/player.cpp) interprets the tens digit of an ammunition action code as its weapon number, and the units digit as the number of portions. The current implementation follows that interpretation, initializes the player with 100 health and a full supply for weapon 1, and uses the same reference's health and armor amounts and caps. These behaviors still need comparison against the original game.

The simulation is independent of rendering and transport. `WeaponDefinitions::parse` reads eight weapon rows from a supplied `chasm.inf`; `Inventory::new` initializes state; `Inventory::pickup` returns `Collected`, `Full`, or `Unsupported` so a future authoritative world can decide whether to remove a pickup. Weapon ownership and ammunition are tracked separately: ammo may be collected before the corresponding weapon. Keys remain collectible when already owned, since the reference triggers map links on key collection. This does not implement placement overlap, pickup respawns, inventory transfer between levels, timed power-ups, damage, weapon firing, or synchronization.

The parser and pickup behavior have synthetic tests and were additionally checked against this installation's actual `chasm.inf`: all eight rows parsed; the first weapon starts with its configured 120-round limit; action 22 adds two configured portions to the second weapon's ammo; action 2 then adds that weapon's configured pickup amount.
