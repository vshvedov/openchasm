# Provisional enemy AI

`EnemyAi` holds per-placement brains separate from actors, the renderer, and transport. Call `tick` once per 60 Hz simulation tick with actor definitions and the authoritative world. Living, difficulty-enabled actors acquire a player within eight tiles when an opaque wall does not interrupt the sight line, turn toward the last known position, and chase it. The movement speed uses the data table's `SPEED / 10` units per second and stops actors whose speed is two or less, following the independent [PanzerChasm monster implementation](https://github.com/Panzerschrek/Chasm-Reverse/blob/master/src/server/monster.cpp); collision radius derives from `WRad / 256`. Dead actors stop updating and difficulty flags disable actors at load.

When within 0.8 tiles and in sight, actors with a positive kick value emit a melee attack event using the definition's kick damage, at most once every 60 ticks. The caller must apply damage, display animation and audio, and decide death and respawn behavior. The caller can also alert an actor to a position after a hit. Actor IDs in events are stable source placement indices. The AI neither performs network communication nor uses a real-time clock.

This is an initial simulation, not a 1:1 reconstruction. Its detection range, line-of-sight handling, attack reach and timing, turning, target persistence, and collision response need comparison against original gameplay. It has no ranged attacks, pathfinding around obstacles, vertical movement, object or actor collisions, wind, sounds, animation state machine, monster-vs-monster targeting, or player death rules. Movement checks walls in bounded substeps and treats 1–86 as opaque for vision and the world's `blocks_movement` property as collision. Transparent and invisible walls do not obstruct sight. These are provisional, particularly around dynamic wall materials and one-sided geometry.
