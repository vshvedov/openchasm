# Reflector and chojin

The session leaves protective pickups disabled by default. An application may call `Session::set_protective_powerups_enabled(true)` before taking its restart snapshot, once it can show the player the effect. With the option off, item action codes 132 and 133 remain available on the map. With it on, they are collected and tracked in `GameInventory::protective_powerups`; cloning the session preserves both timers. New effects apply on the pickup tick, and existing effects decrement at the start of each subsequent live tick. Level carry creates new game state and does not transfer the effects.

The reflector lasts 2400 ticks (40 seconds) and chojin lasts 1800 ticks (30 seconds); subsequent pickups add the corresponding duration using saturating arithmetic. Durations and stacking follow the independent PanzerChasm reconstruction, which marks stacking and chojin behavior as unverified. This fixed-tick interpretation expires when the counter reaches zero, whereas the reconstruction uses a strict greater-than time check.

Chojin prevents both health loss and armor depletion in the shared player damage path. It does not remove the underlying hit or pre-armor explosion event. Knockback, hit flashes and other original reactions are not modeled here.

The reflector redirects an incoming physical projectile on player contact before impact damage or explosion. It retains its ID, speed, remaining lifetime and launch height, changes owner to the player and points radially away from the player; a coincident center reverses the incoming horizontal velocity. The simulation retraces it during the same tick and reports `SessionEvents::projectile_reflections`; the suppressed player impact produces no impact or explosion event. Instant shots, melee and explosions originating elsewhere are not reflected. Reflection is provisional 2D behavior; original vertical physics, homing changes, presentation and multiplayer ownership remain work for the host.
